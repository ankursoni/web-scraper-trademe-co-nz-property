Description

Welcome to 9 Kesteven Avenue - a boutique collection of architecturally designed homes in the heart of Glendowie, offering a rare opportunity to secure brand-new living off-plan.

With two already sold, just four remain - act now to secure your position ahead of completion.

Each home has been thoughtfully designed for modern living, featuring three bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms and light-filled open plan spaces that flow seamlessly to private landscaped outdoor areas. Clean architectural lines, quality materials and a timeless neutral palette create homes that are both stylish and highly liveable on freehold titles.

The ground floor is designed for connection - a central living hub opening to sun-soaked decking and gardens. Upstairs, three well-proportioned bedrooms offer a peaceful retreat, including a luxuriously large master with ensuite.

Designed with longevity in mind, these homes will include premium appliances, off-street parking and low-maintenance finishes - delivering comfort, quality and ease from day one.

Set in one of Auckland's most consistently in-demand eastern suburbs, you're moments from beaches, parks, quality schooling and village amenities - a location that continues to perform.
